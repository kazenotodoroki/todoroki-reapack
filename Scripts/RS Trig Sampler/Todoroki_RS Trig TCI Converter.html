NOTES

Decoding is bit-exact (same audio as Trigger plays, same velocity layers and round robins) and produces exactly the same files as the RS Trig Kit Builder script in REAPER. The Trigger Instrument Editor applies a gain to every sound of a multi-sound .tci: kits keep that level, like Trigger. Large stereo kits (4 x 12 sounds) are about 2 min 30 of audio and ~55 MB on disk each.
